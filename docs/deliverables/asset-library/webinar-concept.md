# Webinar Campaign Concept
_Audience: CFOs, CTOs, enterprise technology leaders · Format: Campaign kit_

> Independent concept created for a portfolio project. Not NVIDIA material; not reviewed or endorsed by NVIDIA. Proof points cite public sources; projections are labeled.

## Title
The Economics of AI Inference: Measuring Cost per Token Before You Scale

## Target audience
Finance and technology leaders at enterprises moving AI from pilots into production.

## Customer pain
AI use is broad, but few organizations can show what each unit of AI output costs or returns [MCK26] [DEL26].

## Description
A 45-minute session for finance and technology leaders on how to measure, model and reduce the cost of AI output, with a walkthrough of an open cost model.

## Learning objectives
1. Define cost per million tokens and cost per productive GPU-hour
2. Read vendor performance claims and their baselines
3. Build a first TCO model with your own assumptions
4. Plan utilization measurement before buying more capacity

## Promotional email
Subject: What does a million tokens cost you?
