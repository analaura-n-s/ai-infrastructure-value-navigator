_Audience: CEO, enterprise technology leaders · Format: 1 page_

## Headline
From AI pilots to measured AI output

## The problem
AI use is broad, but measured value is not. 44% of organizations report scaling AI across the enterprise and 37% report a positive EBIT contribution [MCK26]. About one in five say AI operating costs, including tokens, constrain use [MCK26].

## What changes with rack-scale AI infrastructure
Agentic AI multiplies the number of tokens each task needs. The economics of AI therefore depend on the cost of each unit of output. NVIDIA projects that Vera Rubin NVL72 delivers one-tenth the cost per million tokens of GB200 NVL72 for a reasoning model at long context [VR].

## Three questions to ask before you scale
1. What does a million tokens cost us today, and how would we know?
2. How much of the capacity we already own is in use?
3. Which facility, skills and partner constraints set our timeline?

## Proof points
• One-tenth the cost per million tokens vs GB200 NVL72 (Kimi-K2-Thinking, 32K/8K; projected) [VR]
• Up to 10x more tokens per megawatt vs GB200 NVL72 (projected) [VR]
• Reference design for AI factories released Mar 16, 2026 [DSX]

## Next step
Run your numbers through an open cost model, then review the result with finance and engineering together.
