# Sales Battlecard
_Audience: Sales and partner teams · Format: 1 page, internal_

> Independent concept created for a portfolio project. Not NVIDIA material; not reviewed or endorsed by NVIDIA. Proof points cite public sources; projections are labeled.

## When to use
Enterprise accounts evaluating rack-scale inference capacity where finance is involved.

## Discovery questions
• What does a million tokens cost you today?
• How do you measure GPU utilization now?
• Who signs off the business case?

## Positioning
Lead with unit economics and measurement; support with footnoted performance claims.

## Alternatives the buyer may consider
AMD Instinct MI355X with ROCm [AMD355] [ROCM]; Google Cloud TPU7x (Google Cloud only) [TPU7]; AWS Trainium3 and Inferentia2 (AWS only) [TRN3] [INF]. Do not claim head-to-head performance without a published, comparable benchmark.

## Objection handling
• 'Your multipliers don't apply to us.' Offer a workload-specific evaluation and an open cost model.
• 'We have idle GPUs already.' Start with a utilization assessment; value improves when capacity is used.
• 'Our facility can't host it.' Discuss colocation, cloud and DSX-based planning.

## Do not say
• Unqualified superlatives
• Any figure without its baseline and date
• Availability dates not confirmed in writing
