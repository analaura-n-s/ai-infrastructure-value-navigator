    01 · Executive Overview

    Enterprises are adopting AI faster than they are capturing its value

    Research question: How can NVIDIA translate complex AI infrastructure capabilities into clear, measurable, and audience-specific business value propositions to support enterprise adoption? This tool bridges technical product documentation and commercial decision-making.

    How to read this dashboard. Every statistic below is drawn from a public source, with its publisher, publication date and sample. Select a card to see methodology details. Figures from different surveys measure different things and are not directly comparable. Items marked Hypothesis are the author's interpretations, not research findings.

    The value gap: adoption vs. scaled impact

    Sources: McKinsey, The state of AI in 2025 (Nov 5, 2025; n=1,993, 105 nations) and The state of AI in 2026 (Aug 25, 2026; n=1,719, 97 nations). 2026 AI-use figure is reported only as "nearly nine in ten" and is plotted as 88% for 2025 only. 2025 "scaling" figure is reported in that edition as "one-third"; the 2026 edition cites 38% for the prior year.
